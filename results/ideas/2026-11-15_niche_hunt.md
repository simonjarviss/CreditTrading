# Niche hunt, 2026-11-15 — 32 ideas from six lanes, each attacked by a skeptic

**Written:** 2026-11-15 · **Status:** a ledger of ideas, not results. Nothing here is a
measured return unless a card is named. Full text of every idea and every objection, with
sources and the scouts' [V]/[S]/[U] tags: `results/ideas/niche_hunt/hunt.json`.

## How it was run

The team lead asked on 2026-11-15 for little-known fields, undersized markets and
alternative data, and allowed agent workflows for it. Six scouts each took one lane and
used public sources only: obscure corporate actions; niche listed securities; regulatory
filings as data; data from outside finance; mechanical and calendar flows; options, crypto
and oddities. Each read the desk's idea cards first so as not to repeat them. Three
skeptics then tried to kill every idea: is it already on the desk, does the live source
really list the event in time, has it decayed, can it be traded at Alpaca. Verdicts are the
skeptics': 5 test first, 9 worth a test, 15 weak, 3 dead.

## What I take from it

- The five ranked first are: thrift conversions (tested, above); a recent-share-issuance
  flag as a filter on the small-stock earnings-drift longs; the trade-remedy calendar
  (antidumping decisions, bought through the listed petitioner); unit-trust deposits and
  terminations in closed-end funds, read from EDGAR form 487; and the forced closing trade
  of single-stock leveraged ETFs.
- The issuance filter is the cheapest to test: the follow-on file is already staged.
- The unit-trust flow sits next to the desk's own closed-end fund books and uses a filing
  nobody on the desk has read.
- The closing trade of leveraged single-stock funds needs a decision before the close on
  an intraday price, which the shadow engine does not do.

## Antidumping calendar: closed at the count, 2026-11-16 [V]

The scout and the skeptic agreed on a gate before any return: stop if fewer than about
eight cases a year have a listed petitioner worth under $5bn.

- **Source:** the USITC's public investigations file (`https://ids.usitc.gov/investigations.json`,
  37 MB, 4,446 records, fetched 2026-11-16; kept outside the repository). It does carry
  petitioner names, from 2021 on: 76 preliminary-phase antidumping or countervailing cases
  started 2021–2025 name at least one (10, 7, 12, 27, 20 by year). Before 2021 the field
  is empty, which is what the scout saw.
- **Count:** 11 of the 76 name a company whose name matches a CRSP common stock, and 7 a
  company with a median market value under $5bn in 2021–25 (Eastman Kodak, Leggett & Platt
  and Carpenter, Worthington, Ferroglobe twice, Rayonier Advanced Materials, Manitowoc).
  By year: 0, 0, 3, 1, 3.
- **Why so few:** most petitions are filed by a coalition, a union or a private company.
  The 184 distinct petitioner names are led by the steelworkers' union and by "Coalition
  of …" entries, whose member companies the file does not list.
- **How it was matched:** company names with legal suffixes removed, exact or prefix match
  to CRSP issuer names active from 2021. A rough match: a listed parent filing through a
  differently named subsidiary is missed, and coalition members are not counted.
- **What would reopen it:** member lists for the coalitions (they are in each petition's
  public version), which could lift the count several-fold.

## Patent import cases (section 337): closed at the count, 2026-11-16 [V]

Seen in the same file: a company asks the Commission to bar imports that infringe its
patents, and the case runs on a public dated track (instituted, a judge's initial
determination, a final determination). Not from the hunt; counted before any return.

- **Count** (`results/ideas/usitc_337/stage.py`, CRSP names valid on the event date, 2009–2025):
  1,390 violation-phase investigations; 165 institutions with a complainant matched to a CRSP
  common stock, 71 of them worth under $5bn at the time: about four a year (between 1 and
  10). Judges' rulings on the merits for those small complainants: 21 violation, 16 no
  violation, in 17 years.
- **Verdict:** too few for a book or for a test that could say anything. The same gate as
  the antidumping idea (about eight a year) fails.
- **The match is exact on the reduced name**, so a subsidiary filing under its own name is
  missed. Respondents are more numerous (448 matched institutions, 136 small), but a
  respondent's event is a short.

## The idea

Funds front-run S&P 500 changes. The 600 gets less attention, and a share dropped from the
600 altogether is a small loser that index funds must sell whatever the price. If the selling
overshoots, the share should recover; a share added should give some back. The census of
2026-10-19 (row D8) looked only at the announcement-to-effective run-up and noted that a
post-effective reversal "would be a new idea". The graveyard's `SCR-IDX-REBAL` is about
fund-of-CEF indices, a different mechanism. No return on this had been read on the desk.

## The data limit, which decides what this test is

- Compustat `idxcst_his` on R2 holds **today's members only** (602 / 400 / 503 rows, no end
  dates) [V, re-measured 2026-11-16]. CRSP `dsp500list` is absent. S&P's announcement pages
  refuse robots [S: `results/events/ssx/ssx_data/DATA_MAP_2026-10-19.md`]; Wikipedia's S&P
  600 change table is incomplete [U]. Neither public route was used.
- Membership is therefore read from the **month-end holdings of the index ETFs**. A change is
  known to the month, not the day. The book enters **1 to about 23 sessions after the
  effective date**. A rebound that is over within days of the change is missed. This is a
  later entry than the lane asked for, and it is not that test.
- No announcement dates exist in this source at all.

## What was fixed before the test

Written in `explore.py` on 2026-11-16; only report and holdings counts had been read. Kept
word for word.

- **Change:** in the fund's list at two consecutive month ends and gone at the next (deletion),
  or the reverse (addition). **OUT** = the dropped share is in neither of the other two S&P
  funds; **NEW** = the added share was in neither.
- **Decided** at the first session after the report date; filled at the next close; earns
  from the session after (shift(2), `harness.evaluate`).
- **Eligible:** a US common share that traded that day, price ≥ $1 (a completed merger is gone).
- **Books:** (a) long 600 OUT deletions, 20 and 60 sessions, against IWM; (b) 600 NEW
  additions shown long against IWM, expected negative. Equal weight, flat under 3 live names.
- **Per event:** buy and hold against IWM, t on monthly cohort means.
- **Controls:** C1 the same shares 60 sessions earlier; C2 five size-matched non-members;
  C3 five matched on size and on the prior 126-session return.
- **Expected:** (a) positive and above C3; (b) negative and below C2.

## What I conclude

1. Entered at the month end after the change, there is no rebound in shares dropped from the
   600 and no give-back in shares added, in any era, against IWM or against matched shares.
2. This does **not** test the first days after the effective date. If forced selling
   reverses, the literature's window is days, and a month-end entry is on average about two
   weeks late. The clean reading is: whatever happens is over by then.
3. Not a shadow candidate. Nothing here to tweak without a dated source.

- Daily fund holdings (iShares publishes IJR's daily; the history would have to be bought or
  collected forward) would date changes to the day from 2026 onward.
